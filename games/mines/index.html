❔ How to Play

Open squares until only mines are left. A number tells you how many of the eight squares around it hold a mine.

Tap opens a square. Long-press flags one — or turn on 🚩 mode and tap to flag instead.

Tap a number that already has all its mines flagged to open everything else around it in one go.

No board ever needs a guess. Every board is played by a solver first, and any board that would come down to a coin flip is thrown away and replaced.
