🧩 Neon Cipher

One five-letter word, six guesses. Every guess lights up to tell you how close you are.

laser L is in the word, in the right spot.
pilot I is in the word, wrong spot.
ghost S is not in the word at all.

❔ How to Play

Type any five-letter word and press Enter. The tiles reveal how each letter scores.

a Right letter, right spot.
a Right letter, wrong spot.
a Not in the word.

Repeated letters only light up as many times as they appear in the answer — the extras go grey.

Stuck? 💡 Hint reveals one letter in its real position. The daily gets one; Easy practice gets two.

Any real five-letter word is a legal guess — you are not limited to the words that can be answers.

Daily is the same word for everybody, once a day. Practice is a fresh random word any time you like.

On Hard practice there are no hints, and every clue you have uncovered has to appear in your next guess.
